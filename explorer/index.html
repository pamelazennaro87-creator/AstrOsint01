Geographic scope matters. The second CSV supplied by the project is labelled Taoudeni–Tanezrouft Basin, not the Inner Niger Delta boundary. This explorer preserves the dataset's actual name; it must not be interpreted as a precise measurement of the Inner Niger Delta alone. GRACE has a coarse spatial footprint (roughly 300 km).

How to read this

The line shows the selected monthly anomaly series. The trend is an ordinary least-squares fit across the selected window; it is descriptive and is not, by itself, a significance test or a causal explanation. Always compare the full record with the shorter windows: a recent recovery can coexist with a long-term decline.

Data integrity and GWSa meaning

Where the original monthly value is missing, the repository provides a seasonal-model filled series and a fill flag. Filled values are marked with hollow amber points and excluded from the trend fit. GEOGloWS derives GWSa by subtracting GLDAS soil-moisture, snow and canopy anomalies from TWSa. Surface water is not removed, so rivers, lakes, reservoirs and wetlands can contribute to GWSa. It is a model-derived residual, not a direct well measurement. Read the method.
