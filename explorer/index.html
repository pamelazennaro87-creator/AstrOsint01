How to read this

The line shows the selected monthly anomaly series. The trend is an ordinary least-squares fit across the selected window; it is descriptive and is not, by itself, a significance test or a causal explanation. Always compare the full record with the shorter windows.

What GWSa means here

Groundwater residual (GWSa) is typically derived by subtracting modelled soil-moisture, snow and canopy components from total water storage. Surface water is not fully removed, so rivers, lakes and wetlands can still influence the residual. It is a model-derived estimate, not a direct well measurement.
