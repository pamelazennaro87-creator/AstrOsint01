# ASTROSINT 01 — EARTH IN TRANSITION

**NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026**

---

## Our Mission

We are building an interactive investigative experience that helps people explore environmental changes using real NASA data.

This is **not just another dashboard**.

Users should be able to investigate evidence, compare regions, identify trends, and understand **what is changing, where, and by how much**.

The application is designed to be usable by people with **photosensitive epilepsy**, and includes optional **data sonification** so that trends can be explored through sound.

---

## Our Method

**DETECT → COMPARE → VERIFY → EXPLORE**

We examine NASA datasets, quantify trends, assess statistical significance, and carefully distinguish verified evidence from assumptions.

**Data integrity is non-negotiable.**  
Every conclusion must be traceable to its original source. AI can support exploration, but it must never replace verification.

---

## Accessibility by Design

Scientific evidence should be accessible through:

- Clear visualizations, structured text, and accessible data tables  
- Screen-reader and full keyboard-compatible navigation  
- Optional audio feedback and data sonification  
- Reduced motion and complete avoidance of flashing or strobing effects  

Our goal is to support blind and low-vision users, and to improve safety and usability for people with photosensitive epilepsy. Accessibility features will be tested, not simply declared.

---

## Beyond Observation

Could environmental trends help us anticipate future changes?

We can explore predictive methods **only when the available data supports them**, and we will clearly communicate uncertainty and limitations.

Any future application related to astronaut or medical safety would require separate, rigorous scientific validation.

---

## First Prototype Goals

1. Select a suitable NASA dataset  
2. Compare a variable across two regions over a defined period  
3. Measure trends and assess their statistical significance  
4. Present results through accessible, fully traceable evidence
