01 / The investigation

Two water stories. One investigative method.

We begin with monthly GRACE / GRACE-FO-derived anomalies, then ask what independent evidence is needed to interpret them. This is an exploratory comparison with explicit geographic and methodological limits.

Case A · California, USA

Central Valley

Long-term decline

Representative series shows a negative full-record signal in total-water storage and the model-derived residual. Independent research documents groundwater depletion and land subsidence. These are complementary evidence layers.

Case B · Sahel/Sahara

Taoudeni–Tanezrouft Basin

A much weaker signal

Near-flat to weakly negative series. This basin is not interchangeable with the Inner Niger Delta. Geographic definition and independent verification remain essential.

Critical distinction: GWSa is a model-derived residual that can retain surface-water storage changes. We disclose uncertainty and do not infer causation from a satellite trend alone.

03 / Our method

From detection to investigation

The same analytical sequence is applied to both regions. Every conclusion should be traceable to a source and should communicate uncertainty.

DETECT

Identify the water-storage trends in NASA satellite records.

COMPARE

Use consistent regional definitions and comparable methods.

VERIFY

Cross-check with independent satellite, scientific and public evidence.

EXPLORE

Investigate who controls resources, who benefits and who bears risk.

05 / Accessibility by design

Evidence must be safe to access

Photosensitive safety is not an add-on. It is a core design requirement of AstrOsint01.

No flashing content

We target WCAG 2.3.1 and the stricter 2.3.2 (Three Flashes). No decorative strobing, no high-frequency animations.

Safe Mode (always available)

One click removes remaining transitions and animations. Also respects the system prefers-reduced-motion setting automatically.

Multiple ways to read data

Every chart is paired with a structured data table. Colour is never the only signal.

Keyboard & screen-reader ready

Full navigation without reliance on motion or visual-only cues. Focus states are high-contrast.
