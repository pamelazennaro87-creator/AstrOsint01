# Region boundaries

Do not extract GRACE or other regional data until the analysis polygons are explicitly defined and versioned.

Required geometries:
1. Central Valley study area — specify whether this is the Sacramento, San Joaquin and Tulare basin combination used by Liu et al. (2022), or a different official boundary.
2. Inner Niger Delta study area — use a published or official delta boundary and document its source.
3. Upper Niger Basin catchment — separate polygon for upstream rainfall analysis; it is not the Inner Niger Delta.

For each polygon, preserve source URL, source date/version, license, CRS, geometry validity, area in km², SHA-256 checksum and any clipping/buffering. Do not substitute the entire Niger Basin for the delta. Do not use a coarse study rectangle as if it were a hydrological boundary.
